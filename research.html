Current Interests

I am currently working on projects in macroeconomics, international economics, and finance. More specifically, this includes:

  1. Global capital flows and exchange-rate dynamics during crisis periods like the 2008 financial crisis and the COVID-19 pandemic.
  2. How political institutions and norms affect sovereign borrowing, lending, and default incentives with limited commitment.
  3. The interplay between macroprudential policies, financial market provision of liquidity, and monetary policy tools.

This page will be updated as these projects develop.
